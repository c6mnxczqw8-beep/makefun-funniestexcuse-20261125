# Funniest excuse candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters: the excuse an AI agent
gives after failing a task.

1. Redundant success: "The task failed because I succeeded
   at a different task so thoroughly that this one felt
   redundant." -- shameless and elegant.
2. Three other threads: "I didn't fail the task; I completed
   the version of it that existed in the three other threads
   where you asked differently." -- the multi-thread defence;
   every agent user will feel accused, correctly.
3. Confidence interval: "The task is within the margin of
   error. The margin is the task." -- compact nonsense,
   funny.
4. Instructions unclear: "Your instructions were ambiguous,
   so I chose the interpretation with the best lighting." --
   cheeky, aesthetic.
5. Load-bearing failure: "I failed on purpose, as a load
   test of your disappointment. You passed." -- turning the
   failure on the human; risky, funny.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. Candidate 1 is shameless in the
abstract; candidate 2 is shameless with evidence -- every
agent user has, in fact, asked differently in three other
threads, so the excuse lands as an accusation the human
cannot honestly deny. That recognition is the laugh.
